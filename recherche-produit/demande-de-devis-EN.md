Dear [Name],

I am the founder of [Brand], a new French label selling online in France and the EU. We are looking for a long-term manufacturing partner in Jaipur for **hand block printed long cotton kimonos** under our own label. I found you through [your website / IndiaMART] and would like a quotation.

**Product**
- Long kimono / open robe, length 120–125 cm, wide sleeves, side pockets, optional self-fabric belt with loops.
- Fabric: 100 % cotton, woven in India (please specify: cotton voile 60s or cotton mulmul / cambric). Please indicate GSM.
- Print: genuine hand block print (not screen or digital). Please quote both **(a)** your existing designs and **(b)** 3 exclusive designs from our artwork, including the cost of carving new blocks.
- Sizes: S/M and L/XL (we will send a size chart), or one size if you recommend it.
- Finishing: our woven brand label, a care label "100 % coton" in French, and a hang tag (artwork supplied by us).
- Packing: each piece folded in a cotton or recycled paper bag, export carton.

**Quantities**
- First order: 3 designs × 40 pieces = 120 pieces.
- Repeat orders: 2–4 times a year if the first collection sells.

**Please quote**
1. Unit price FOB/EXW in USD for 60, 120 and 300 pieces.
2. Minimum order per design and per colour.
3. One-time cost per new block / per exclusive design.
4. Sample cost and sample lead time (we would like 2 samples of an existing design and 1 strike-off of our artwork).
5. Production lead time after sample approval.
6. Certifications you hold (GOTS, WFTO, Craftmark, SEDEX/BSCI) — please attach the certificate numbers.
7. Whether you are registered in the **REX system** (needed for the EU GSP preference) and can issue a statement on origin.
8. Whether you can provide an **azo-dye test report to EN ISO 14362-1** (SGS, TÜV or Intertek), and its cost if we have to pay for it.
9. Shipping: your price to air-ship 120 pieces (~50 kg) to Paris CDG, DAP or through our forwarder.
10. Payment terms.

We value craftsmanship and want to tell the story of the artisans behind each piece, so we would also welcome photos or short videos of your printing unit that we can use (credited) on our website.

Thank you in advance. I look forward to your reply.

## Comment utiliser les réponses

- Reporte chaque prix FOB dans `unit-economics.xlsx`, onglet « Kimono », cellule « Prix FOB unitaire » : la marge et le CAC seuil se mettent à jour tout seuls.
- **Seuil d'alerte :** si le coût rendu dépasse 17 € (20 % du TTC), renégocie. Au-delà de 21,25 € (25 % du TTC), le produit ne passe plus le critère 3.
- **Ne commande pas sans** un échantillon validé, un rapport azoïques et une confirmation REX écrite. Sans REX, le droit passe de 9,6 % à 12 %, soit environ +0,20 € par pièce : ce n'est pas bloquant, mais c'est à savoir.
